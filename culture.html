Culture & community

Tropical culture of islands

More than beaches

Maldivian cultural life reflects island communities, Dhivehi language, maritime traditions, local cuisine, craftsmanship and Islamic faith. Visitors can explore local markets, mosques, and community events to gain insight into the daily lives of Maldivians.

Visitors to inhabited islands should respect community norms, religious spaces and local expectations around clothing and photography. Engaging with local guides and participating in cultural experiences can enrich your understanding of the Maldives beyond its natural beauty.
