A friendly introduction to the ingredients and dishes of the Maldives.

Island food traditions

Fish, coconut and starches appear in many traditional Maldivian dishes. Meals reflect a long relationship with the sea and the islands' trading history. Local cuisine is often spicy, and meals are typically shared with family and friends.

Examples include, garudhiya, a fish broth commonly served with rice or roshi. Fihunu mas, grilled fish seasoned with chili and spices. And hedhikaa, a variety of sweet and savory snacks, often enjoyed with tea. Coconut milk and grated coconut are frequently used in curries and desserts, adding richness and flavor to the dishes.

Ask about ingredients and allergies before ordering; preparation varies by household and restaurant.
