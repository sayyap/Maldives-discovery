Marine life

Discover reef ecosystems and simple ways to protect them.

A living underwater environment

Coral reefs support fish and many other marine organisms. Their health can be affected by warming seas, pollution and physical damage.

Observe wildlife from a distance, avoid touching coral, never feed animals and follow the guidance of qualified local operators.

Responsible ocean checklist

Look, do not touch

Keep hands and fins away from coral.

Reduce waste

Carry reusable bottles and dispose of litter responsibly.

Respect wildlife

Do not chase or crowd marine animals.
