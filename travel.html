Travel tips

A practical checklist for thoughtful, prepared visitors.

Before your journey

Confirm passport, visa and arrival requirements through official channels. Rules and transport arrangements can change, so do not rely on an old travel blog for final decisions.

This guide is educational and does not provide live schedules, visa decisions, prices or travel bookings.

Simple planning checklist

How should I plan island transfers?

Check whether your accommodation arranges a speedboat, domestic flight or other transfer, and confirm operating days and departure times directly.

What should I pack?

Bring sun protection, light clothing, a reusable water bottle and suitable attire for inhabited islands.

How can I travel responsibly?

Respect local rules, minimize single-use plastic and choose operators that prioritize safety and environmental care.
